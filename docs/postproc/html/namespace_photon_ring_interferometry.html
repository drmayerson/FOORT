float PaddingFactor = 25,
bool Verbose = True,
float sample_spacing = 1.0 )

Calculates the complex visibility from a given radon transform.

Parameters
FOORTRadonThe radon transform to calculate the complex visibility from.
PaddingFactorThe factor to pad the radon transform by (default = 25).
VerboseWhether to print out progress information (default = True).
sample_spacingThe spacing between samples in the radon transform (default = 1.0).
Returns
complvis: The complex visibility of the radon transform.
freqs: The frequencies of the FFT of the radon transform.

The complex visibility is calculated by taking the FFT of the radon transform, shifting it to center the zero frequency, and selecting only the positive frequencies.

Note
The padding factor is used to increase the resolution of the FFT.
The sample spacing is used to calculate the frequencies of the FFT. It corresponds to the pixel width in the original image.
